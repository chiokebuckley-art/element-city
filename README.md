# Element City

Play at https://chiokebuckley-art.github.io/element-city/

## Version 0.7.0: vocabulary and recipe bridges

Build a city first. Start with small material models, a twelve-element shelf, and a simple park-water delivery. Then open Learn to prepare measured city orders.

The new saved practice path covers measuring, fractions, decimals, percentages, material balance, concentrations, ingredient jobs, process order, and equipment settings. It leads into the existing school paste, clinic supply, and park treatment recipes. Two independent changed loads complete each step; hints and corrected answers remain useful practice without counting as independent success.

New words have plain explanations, concrete metaphors, and optional deeper science. Unfinished practice, word introductions, and recipe progress survive reloads. Detailed material science and the full science table remain optional. Existing cities and Lab drafts are preserved.

Existing beginner players: finish the first park-water delivery, then open Learn. Experienced players can keep the existing workshop or choose the beginner city path in Settings.

This repository contains the published browser game. Editable source, tests, and research remain in the `element-city/` folder of the private `command-center` repository (PR #5).

The advanced chemistry path remains available. Practice completion is not certification of chemistry or engineering mastery. Everything runs in the browser, with no account, purchases, or paid services. Simulations are educational, not instructions for making products for use or sale. Park water is not drinking water.

Art, geometry and sound are original (CC0). Fonts Nunito and Fredoka (SIL OFL 1.1). OCR by tesseract.js with tessdata_fast English (Apache-2.0).
